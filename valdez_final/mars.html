Mars: The Red Planet

Mars surface is covered in iron oxide, which gives the planet its unique red color.

Mars is home to the tallest volcano in our solar system, Olympus Mons.

Mars Fun Facts

Seasons

Like Earth, Mars has seasons that last twice as long because of its longer orbit around the sun.

Atmosphere

Mars has a thin atmosphere composed of mainly carbon dioxide, with dust storms that can last months and cover the entire planet.

Gravity

The gravity on Mars is about 38% of Earth's gravity.
